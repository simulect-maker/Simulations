Reaction Definition

A nucleophilic substitution is a type of organic reaction in which a strong nucleophile replaces a weak leaving group attached to a carbon atom.

What is a Nucleophile?

A substance that is rich in electrons and wants to form a bond by donating an electron pair.

Based on the reaction pathway, it is classified into:

SN1

Substitution Nucleophilic Unimolecular

SN2

Substitution Nucleophilic Bimolecular
